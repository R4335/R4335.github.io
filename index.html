About

I work on Linux systems close to the kernel.

My current focus is Linux kernel development and Linux security—understanding how operating systems behave at a low level and how vulnerabilities emerge when abstractions fail.

Most of my work revolves around C, Linux internals, reverse engineering, and building pwn/reversing CTF challenges.
